About the Institute

Experienced faculty. Energetic teaching. Real results.

Located in the heart of Adajan at Om Shopping Centre, Education Point has become a trusted name for students and parents across Surat. Our teaching approach blends concept clarity with consistent practice and regular feedback.

  • Concept-first teaching — fundamentals explained clearly before moving ahead.
  • Small batches — so every student gets individual attention and doubt-solving time.
  • Regular tests & feedback — progress tracked and shared with parents.
  • Easy to reach — opposite Prime Arcade, beside Malaviya Hospital, Adajan.
